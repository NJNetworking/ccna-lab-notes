### Which of the following channel-group mode combinations will result in an operational EtherChannel? Pick three answers. ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* on-on
* passive-passive
* desirable-auto
* auto-auto
* active-desireable
* on-desireable
* active-active

##### MY ANSWER: a) on-on c) desirable-auto g) active-active ######

### In the output of the ```do show etherchannel summary``` command, you notice that the physical interfaces in the EtherChannel you configured have the (P) flag next to them. What does this mean? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* The interfaces are in LACP passive mode
* The interfaces are bundled in the port-channel
* The interfaces are paused until the other switch's interface channel is configured
* The interfaces are L2 Etherchannel

##### MY ANSWER: b) The interfaces are bundled in portchannel #####
