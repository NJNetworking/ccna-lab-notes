### What kind of cable is used to connect to a Cisco device via the RJ45 console port? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Rollover cable
* Crossover cable

##### MY ANSWER: a) Rollover cable  ######

### You type ```enable``` to enter pExec mode on your Cisco router, but the password isn't accepted. What could be the problem? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* ```service password-encryption``` is enabled
* ```service password-encrpytion``` is disabled
* You have CAPSLOCK on 

##### MY ANSWER: c) You have CAPSLOCK on #####
