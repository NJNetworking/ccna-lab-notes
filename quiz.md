### There is a duplex mismatch between Switch1's Fast Ethernet 0/1 interface and Switch2's Fast Ethernet 0/1 interface, which are connected. Autonegotation is disabled. What will be the result. ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Improved performance
* Collision will occur
* SW1 will sense SW2's duplex and adjust to match

##### MY ANSWER: b) Collision will occur  ######

### What is used on half-duplex interfaces to detect and avoid collisions? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* CSMA/CD
* CSMA/CA
* Autonegotation
* Duplex-auto

##### MY ANSWER: a) CSMA/CD #####

### Which command shows various counters of errors detected on an interface? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* ```show interfaces```
* ```show ip interface brief```
* ```show interfaces status```
* ```show interface errors```

##### MY ANSWER: a) ```show interfaces```  #####

### Which are examples of errors that may occur on a network interface? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Runts, Giants, Broadcasts
* Packets, Frames, Bytes, Inputs
* Shorts, Longs, Oversized
* Runts, Giants, CRC

##### MY ANSWER: d) Runts, Giants, CRC #####

### Switch1 is trying to autonegotiate interface speed settings with Switch2. However, autonegotation is off on SW2's interface. SW2's interface is config with a speed of 100 mbit/s and full duplex. What speed and duplexing will SW1 use assuming it succeeds in sensing the speed? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Speed: 100 Mbps, Duplex: Full
* Speed: 100 Mbps, Duplex: Half
* Speed: 10 Mbps, Duplex: Full
* Speed: 10 Mbps, Duplex: Half
  
##### MY ANSWER: b) 100 Mbps, Duplex: half #####
