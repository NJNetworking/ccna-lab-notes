### You connect two old routers together using a UTP cable, however data is not succesfully sent and received between them. What could be the problem? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* They are connected with a Straight-through cable
* They are connected with a Crossover cable
* They are operating using auto-MDIX

##### MY ANSWER: a) Using Straight-through cable ######

### Your company wants to keep costs down while connecting switches in two seperate buildings that are about 150 meters apart. What type of cable should they use? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* UTP
* SMF
* MMF

##### MY ANSWER: c) Multimode Fiber #####

### Your company wants to connect two offices that are about 3 kilometers apart. They want to keep costs down. What type of cable should they use? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* UTP
* SMF
* MMF

##### MY ANSWER: b) Single-mode fiber #####

### A switch has the following indication over it's network interfaces (Ports are Auto-MDIX). What would happen if you used a straight-through cable to an identical nodetype? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* They would operate normally
* They would operate at a reduced speed
* They couldn't communicate

##### MY ANSWER: a) They would operate normally because of Auto-MDIX enabling #####

### Your company wants to connect many endpoints to a switch which is in a wiring cabinet on the same office floor as the hosts. What kind of cable shold they use? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* UTP
* Single-mode fiber
* Multi-mode fiber

##### MY ANSWER: a) Unshielded twistedpair Rj45 #####
