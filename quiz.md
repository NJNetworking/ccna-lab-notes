### What is the fixed binary value of the first field for an IPv4 header? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* 0010
* 0110
* 0001
* 0100

##### MY ANSWER: d) 0100 = 4  ######

### Which field causes the packet to be dropped if it has a value of 0? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* TTL
* DSCP
* IHL
* ECN

##### MY ANSWER: a) TTL #####

### How are errors in IPv4 packet's encapsulated data detected? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* The IPv4 Checksum field checks for errors
* The encapsulated protocol (tcp/udp) checks for errors
* Errors cannot be detected

##### MY ANSWER: b) TCP/UDP  #####

### Which field of an IPv4 is variable in length? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Options
* Header Checksum
* Total Length
* IHL

##### MY ANSWER: a) Options 0-320 bits #####

### Which bit will be sent to 1 on all IPv4 Packet fragments except the last fragment? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Fragment offset bit
* More fragments bit
* Don't fragment bit
* Packet fragment bit

##### MY ANSWER: b) More fragments bit #####
