### Your company wants to purchase some network hardware to which they can plug in the 30 PC's within your department. Which type of networking device is appropriate for this usecase? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* A router
* A firewall
* A switch
* A server

##### MY ANSWER: c) A SWITCH ######

### You received a video file from your friend's Apple iPhone using AirDrop. What was his iPhone functioning as in that transaction? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* A server
* A client
* A LAN

##### MY ANSWER: a) A SERVER #####

### What is your computer or smartphone acting as while you watch a video on YouTube? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* A server
* An end host
* A client

##### MY ANSWER: c) A CLIENT #####

### Your company wants to purchase some network hardware, to connect its seperate networks together. What network device will accomplish this? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* A firewall
* A host
* A lan
* A router

##### MY ANSWER: d) A ROUTER #####

### Your company wants to upgrade its old firewall that has been in use for several years to one that provides more advanced functions. What kind of firewall should they purchase? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* A host-based firewall
* A next-level firewall
* A NGFW
* A top-layer firewall

##### MY ANSWER: c) A NGFW #####
