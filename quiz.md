
# Quiz 5 & 6 #

---

### Which field of an Ethernet frame provides receiver clock synchronization? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Preamble
* SFD
* Type/length
* FCS

##### MY ANSWER: a) Preamble  ######

### How long is the physical address of a network device ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* 32 bytes
* 32 bits
* 48 bytes
* 48 bits

##### MY ANSWER: d) 48 bits #####

### What is the OUI of this MAC address? E8BA.7011.2874 ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* E8BA
* E8BA.70
* 7011
* E8BA.7011

##### MY ANSWER: b) E8BA.70  #####

### Which field of an Ethernet frame does a switch use to populate its MAC address table? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Preamble
* Length
* Source MAC
* Destination mAC

##### MY ANSWER: c) Source MAC #####

### What kind of frame does a switch flood out of all interfaces except the one it was received on? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Unknown unicast
* Known unicast
* Allcast

##### MY ANSWER: a) Unknown unicast #####

### You send a 36-byte ping to another computer and perform a packet capture to analyze the network traffic. You notice a long series of bytes of 00000000 at the end of the payload. How can you explain these zeroes? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Pings are a series of zeroes
* They are the padding bytes
* They are the FCS

##### MY ANSWER: b) Padding bytes  ######

### Which of these messages is sent to all hosts on the local network? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* ARP request
* ARP reply
* ICMP echo request
* IMCP echo reply

##### MY ANSWER: a) ARP request #####

### Which fields are present in the output of the `show mac address-table` command on a Cisco switch? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* MAC Address, Ports
* VLAN, MAC, Ports
* VLAN, MAC, Type, Ports
* Internet Address, Physical Address, Type

##### MY ANSWER: c) VLAN, MAC, Type, Ports  #####

### Which type of frames does a switch send out of all interfaces, except the one it was received on? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* Broadcast, unknown unicast
* Broadcast, known unicast
* Known unicast, unknown unicast
* Broadcast, unknown unicast, known unicast

##### MY ANSWER: a) Broadcast, unknown unicast #####

### Which command is used on a Cisco switch to clear all dynamic MAC addresses on a specific interface from the MAC address table? ###

#### POSSIBLE ANSWERS ####

* `clear mac address-table interface interface-id`
* `clear mac-address-table dynamic interface interface-id`
* `clear mac-address table dynamic interface interface-id`
* `clear mac address-table dynamic interface interface-id`

##### MY ANSWER: d) `clear mac address-table dynamic interface interface-id` #####
